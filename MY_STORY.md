# My Story - Full Testimony & Calling

This document contains the complete, unedited story of my faith journey and calling to missions. Use this as a reference for future content, blog posts, speaking engagements, and updates to the website.

---

## Foundation: How the Gospel Entered My Family

My dad and his entire side of the family were non-believers who worshiped idols. My mom, on the other hand, grew up in a Christian home and was a believer.

The story of how the Gospel entered my family is one of extraordinary courage and obedience. My dad's brother - my uncle - heard the Gospel, believed in Jesus, and then did something remarkable. He went to my dad's home and **replaced all the idols with Bibles**.

Think about what that meant. He risked being disowned by the family. He could have been beaten. He faced potential rejection from everyone he loved. But he chose obedience to God over fear of consequences. He shared the Gospel with my entire family, not just my dad.

That's how my dad came to faith in Jesus. That's how God's living Word entered our lives and changed everything.

I didn't fully understand the weight of my uncle's sacrifice until he passed away in early 2023. His death made me realize: there are believers around the world who risk their lives, their families, their safety to proclaim the name of Jesus. Sharing the Gospel is worth any cost.

---

## Childhood: Seeds of Service

Growing up in rural India, I watched my parents model generous love every single week.

Every Sunday after church, my mom would prepare meal packages for about 10 homeless people in our area. Then my dad and I would hop on a motorcycle and deliver those packages. I was just a kid, but those Sunday rides taught me something profound: **if we are able to give, we should not hesitate, because God always meets our needs** (Matthew 6:26-30, Philippians 4:19).

At the same time, we were poor. I remember traveling miles on that motorcycle to carry water pots for our own family. We depended on others to help us with water. Sometimes we had to go great distances just to get clean water.

**Fast forward to Honduras, September 2025:** I found myself serving on a water truck, providing clean water to villagers in need. I stood there, once a receiver, now a giver, and I witnessed God's faithfulness firsthand. He is the provider. He lifts us up so we can lift others.

---

## College: Feeding Children, Discovering a Calling

During my college years, I started a non-profit organization dedicated to helping and feeding children. I couldn't share the Gospel openly in that context, but the work satisfied my soul deeply. There was something about meeting people's physical needs that felt right, even though I sensed something was missing.

Then I moved to the United States for further studies, and everything changed.

---

## The Gospel of Grace: Edmond First Baptist Church, Oklahoma

While attending college in the US, I started going to **Edmond First Baptist Church in Oklahoma**. It was there that I heard and truly understood the **Gospel of grace** for the first time in my life.

I had grown up in church. I attended every Sunday. I knew Bible stories. But I didn't know the core message of Christianity:

**Salvation is a gift received through faith in Jesus Christ alone - not earned by works** (Ephesians 2:8-9).

This revelation transformed everything. I finally understood that I didn't have to DO anything to earn eternal life. Jesus already did it all on the cross. I just had to believe.

---

## Full Circle: Sharing the Gospel with My Parents (2024-2025)

Late in 2024, I was in the kitchen with my mom at dinnertime. I asked her a simple question:

**"Mom, why would God let you into Heaven?"**

Her answer broke my heart. She gave a works-based response - she thought she had to earn her way to Heaven through good deeds and religious actions.

I had to lovingly correct her, pointing to Scripture. I showed her Ephesians 2:8-9. I explained the Gospel of grace to my own mother - the woman who brought me to church my whole life.

I had a similar conversation with my dad in early 2025. Same issue. Works-based salvation instead of grace through faith.

And I had the same conversation with my best friend in early 2025.

That's when it hit me like a ton of bricks:

**I cannot assume everyone knows the true Gospel, even people who grew up in church. Even people who have been Christians their whole lives.**

God used me to share the Good News with the very people who brought me to church. Full circle. And that experience ignited my passion for missions, because if my own parents didn't fully understand grace, how many others are out there trusting in their works instead of Jesus?

---

## The Call to Missions (2025)

One Sunday, my pastor was sharing about missionaries serving in other countries. As I listened, I felt deeply convicted by the Holy Spirit.

I dedicated my life to missions that day.

But obedience took time. I didn't go on my first mission trip until **July 2025** - to Paraguay. I was slow to act on what God was calling me to do, but eventually, I obeyed.

---

## Answered Prayer: Honduras (September 2025)

Before my Honduras trip in September 2025, I prayed specifically to God: **"Lord, let me witness at least one salvation."**

God answered that prayer powerfully.

During the trip, a woman heard the Gospel and came to faith in Christ. I watched as tears streamed down her face - tears of repentance, tears of joy, tears of the Holy Spirit moving in her heart. She believed. She was saved.

Witnessing that moment of transformation reminded me of 1 Corinthians 10:31: **"So whether you eat or drink or whatever you do, do it all for the glory of God."**

This wasn't about me. This wasn't about my efforts or my words. This was about God's glory and His power to save. He saves. I'm just obedient.

---

## My Calling: Generous Giving + Bold Gospel Proclamation

Today, I am called to two things:

1. **Give generously to the needy** - food, water, clothing, medical care, whatever is needed
2. **Share the Gospel boldly** - proclaiming the Good News of Jesus without fear

Why am I not afraid? Because of Luke 12:11-12:

> "When you are brought before synagogues, rulers and authorities, do not worry about how you will defend yourselves or what you will say, for the Holy Spirit will teach you at that time what you should say."

I trust that the Holy Spirit will give me the words I need when I need them. And it brings me **so much joy** to serve people and share the Gospel.

---

## My Mission Work: Humanitarian Aid + Gospel Proclamation

I'm most passionate about combining **humanitarian work with Gospel proclamation**. I don't want to just feed people or provide medical care. I want to meet their physical needs AND share the Good News of Jesus.

My role on mission trips is flexible - I serve wherever I'm needed on any given day:
- Medical support
- Logistics and distribution
- Water delivery
- Village evangelism
- Youth ministry

But no matter what role I'm filling, I **keep my heart and ears open to share the Gospel**. If God puts me in a situation, I believe He has prepared someone there to hear - whether that's hearing my testimony or hearing the message of salvation for the first time.

---

## My Philosophy: Partnership, Not Solo Missions

I don't believe in going solo. I believe in **partnering with local believers** who are already on the ground, already serving, already building relationships.

God has shown me that I don't have to be present for the work to continue. He provides the workers. He prepares the hearts. He brings the harvest. My role is simply obedience and faithfulness.

This was clear in Paraguay, where I partnered with a local church planter. It was clear in Honduras, where I worked with BMDMI and local pastors. And it will be clear in Southeast Asia, where I'll partner with missionaries already serving in spiritually opposed regions.

---

## Divine Appointments, Not Accidents

One of my core beliefs is this:

**God orchestrates divine appointments. They are not accidents. They are not destiny or fate. They are intentional encounters where God is already at work.**

When I share the Gospel with someone, it's because God arranged that meeting. When a woman in Honduras comes to faith with tears streaming down her face, it's because God prepared her heart before I ever arrived.

My job is to show up, be obedient, and trust that God is always at work behind the scenes.
